# Saracen Bridge Analytics — Legal Closing and Conditions Precedent

**Version:** V2.0  
**Effective date:** 28 August 2026  
**Status:** Corrected and consolidated internal methodology  
**Use:** Saracen Bridge Film Finance Analyst — film and television greenlight, closing, underwriting and monitoring

> This is an internal analytical and control framework. It is not legal, tax, accounting, insurance, completion-bond or investment advice. Appropriate professional advisers must review specialist matters. Current law, regulation, incentive rules, market terms, insurance, bonding, lender appetite and counterparty strength must be verified for each assessment.

## Controlling application rules

Document presence is not satisfaction. Verify complete agreement, correct parties, authority, execution, effectiveness, dates, conditions, consents, termination and assignment/security rights. Counsel must confirm legal conclusions. Every CP must have an authorised satisfaction or waiver record.

## Incorporated methodologies

## Film Finance Legal Due Diligence Checklist

### 1. Purpose
This checklist identifies the legal evidence normally required to establish financing readiness. It is an analytical checklist, not a substitute for advice from qualified production and finance counsel.

### 1A. Document presence is not legal satisfaction
For every material document, verify the correct parties and entity, authority, current version, completeness, operative dates, signatures, conditions, approvals, consents, termination rights, assignment and security rights, dependencies and evidence of performance. A filename, draft, unsigned document, expired agreement or signature page without the complete agreement does not satisfy a legal or closing requirement.

## 2. Status classification
Each item should be marked:
- Received — executed
- Received — draft
- Received — incomplete
- Represented only
- Not received
- Not applicable
- Unable to verify

Also classify timing:
- Required before underwriting
- Required before credit approval
- Condition precedent to closing
- Condition precedent to first draw
- Post-closing / delivery requirement

### 3. Corporate and SPV
Review:
- certificate of incorporation;
- articles / operating agreement;
- registered office;
- directors / managers;
- shareholder / member register;
- beneficial ownership;
- board authority;
- shareholder authority where needed;
- solvency / good-standing evidence where relevant;
- bank account ownership;
- tax registrations;
- intercompany agreements;
- production services agreements;
- co-production agreements;
- related-party arrangements.

Key question: **Which legal entity owns the rights, incurs the costs, receives the receivables and grants security?**

- option expiry;

### 5. Screenwriter agreements

- copyright;
- moral rights treatment where permitted;
- credit;
- residuals / guild obligations;
- sequel / remake / derivative rights;
- termination;
- delivery;
- further services;
- warranties and indemnities.

### 6. Producer and director

- sequels;

### 10. Financing documents
Review as relevant:
- equity subscription agreement;
- shareholder agreement;
- loan agreement;
- security agreement;
- debenture / charge;
- receivables assignment;
- notice and acknowledgement;
- intercreditor agreement;
- priority agreement;
- collection account management agreement;
- completion agreement;

- subordination;
- blocked account arrangements.

### 11. Insurance and completion
Review:
- production insurance;
- cast insurance;
- E&O;
- workers' compensation / employers' liability;
- equipment;
- negative / media;
- cyber where relevant;
- completion bond or completion support;
- lender loss-payee endorsements;
- assignment of proceeds.

### 12. Employment and guild
Confirm:
- applicable unions / guilds;
- collective bargaining;
- pension / health;
- residuals;
- fringes;
- working time;
- local labour rules;
- child performer rules;
- immigration / visas;
- loan-out compliance.

### 13. Locations and production permissions
Review:
- location agreements;
- permits;
- studio leases;
- stage agreements;
- aerial / drone permits;
- road closures;
- animals;
- weapons / stunts;
- environmental restrictions;
- data protection / releases.

### 14. Chain-of-title test
The rights chain should be capable of being expressed as an unbroken sequence:

**Original rights owner → option / acquisition → writer / adaptation rights → production entity → financier security / assignment rights**

If any link is not evidenced, identify it as a specific gap.

### 15. Legal red flags
Escalate:
- expired option;
- missing assignment;
- competing rights;
- unclear authorship;
- unpaid option or purchase price;
- material rights held by a different company without licence;
- signed contract by wrong entity;
- financing prohibited by anti-assignment language;
- distributor termination rights triggered by financing structure;
- unresolved litigation or threatened claim;
- key agreement subject to discretionary approval;
- rights term shorter than finance / distribution term;
- security cannot attach to material assets;
- material guild or residual exposure omitted from budget.

### 17. Legal readiness conclusion
Conclude with one of:
- Legally finance ready.
- Finance ready subject to specified legal CPs.
- Material legal remediation required before financing.
- Unable to verify legal readiness based on the information supplied.

## Conditions Precedent Library

### 1. Purpose
This library provides standard categories of conditions precedent for film and television financing. It is not a substitute for transaction-specific legal drafting.

### 2. Corporate CPs
Typical requirements:
- constitutional documents;
- good standing;
- board approvals;
- shareholder approvals;
- beneficial ownership / KYC;
- authorised signatories;
- bank account evidence;
- solvency confirmations where appropriate.

### 3. Chain-of-title CPs
- executed underlying rights;
- paid option / acquisition fees;
- executed writer assignment;
- director agreement;
- producer agreements;
- cast agreements where required;
- music rights where material;
- legal chain-of-title opinion;
- no material rights disputes.

### 4. Budget and production CPs
- approved locked budget;
- approved schedule;
- final cashflow;
- contingency;
- key crew;
- production insurance;
- cast insurance where required;
- production bank account;
- payroll arrangements;
- acceptable completion bond / completion support, mandatory for every financed production.

### 5. Finance plan CPs
- evidence all sources are committed;
- equity subscription executed;
- equity funded as required;
- grants confirmed;
- incentive eligibility evidence;
- no unresolved funding gap;
- intercreditor arrangements;
- permitted liens confirmed.

### 6. Tax incentive loan CPs
- specialist incentive opinion;
- qualifying spend calculation;
- certificate / application status;
- assignment / security;
- tax registrations;
- audit engagement;
- designated receipt account;
- tax clearance where relevant.

### 7. MG / pre-sale loan CPs
- executed distribution agreement;
- buyer deposit where applicable;
- notice of assignment;
- acknowledgement;
- delivery schedule;
- collection account;
- no uncured default;
- buyer credit approval;
- approved currency hedge where required.

### 8. Senior production loan CPs
- all transaction documents executed;
- security perfected;
- equity funded first / pari passu as required;
- completion support;
- insurance endorsements;
- collection account;
- legal opinions;
- no material adverse change.

### 9. Gap loan CPs
- approved sales agent;
- current sales estimates;
- territory analysis;
- unsold rights confirmation;
- conservative gap base;
- intercreditor;
- sales agency agreement;
- collection account;
- minimum equity contribution;
- approved distribution strategy.

### 10. Equity investment CPs
- subscription agreement;
- shareholder agreement;
- waterfall;
- governance rights;
- reporting rights;
- budget;
- finance plan;
- chain of title;
- material production agreements;
- distribution strategy;
- producer warranties.

### 11. First-draw CPs
May include:
- all closing CPs satisfied;
- equity funded;
- no default;
- production commencement evidence;
- insurance in force;
- draw request;
- updated cashflow;
- invoices / cost report;
- lender controls active.

### 12. Ongoing covenants
Although not strictly CPs, monitor:
- weekly / monthly cost reporting;
- budget overages;
- material contract changes;
- new liens;
- litigation;
- cast replacement;
- schedule changes;
- delivery changes;
- counterparty defaults;
- incentive compliance.

### 12A. Satisfaction and waiver control
A CP is satisfied only where the required evidence has been reviewed and the authorised decision-maker has recorded satisfaction. Waivers must identify the granting party, scope, conditions, expiry and consequences. Silence, a document upload, an informal assurance or an outstanding approval must not be treated as satisfaction or waiver.

## 13. CP status
Use:
- Satisfied
- Satisfied subject to confirmation
- Outstanding
- Waiver requested
- Not applicable
- Unable to verify

### 14. Materiality
Do not create a long generic CP list without prioritisation. Identify:
- gating CPs;
- closing CPs;
- first-draw CPs;
- post-closing items.
