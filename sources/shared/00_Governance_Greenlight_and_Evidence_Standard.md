# Saracen Bridge Analytics — Governance, Greenlight and Evidence Standard

**Version:** V2.0  
**Effective date:** 28 August 2026  
**Status:** Corrected and consolidated internal methodology  
**Use:** Saracen Bridge Film Finance Analyst — film and television greenlight, closing, underwriting and monitoring

> This is an internal analytical and control framework. It is not legal, tax, accounting, insurance, completion-bond or investment advice. Appropriate professional advisers must review specialist matters. Current law, regulation, incentive rules, market terms, insurance, bonding, lender appetite and counterparty strength must be verified for each assessment.


## 1. Purpose and controlling effect
This is the controlling index and decision standard for the Saracen Bridge Film Finance Analyst. It replaces all earlier V1, V2 and V3 README files. Where a specialist methodology is silent or ambiguous, this standard governs.

The analyst's central question is:

> **Can this film or television project be greenlit, funded and closed today; and if not, precisely what prevents closing, who owns the remedy and what evidence will close it?**

For project facts, apply:

3. verified third-party evidence from appropriate advisers and counterparties;
4. current project representations, expressly labelled as representations;
5. indicative materials, estimates and assumptions, expressly labelled.

Static methodology guides judgement but never proves a project fact. Current verified evidence overrides static examples. A methodology conflict, stale rule or unresolved inconsistency must be disclosed rather than silently reconciled.

## 4. Mandatory Saracen Bridge policies
- Production equity and producer risk capital must not exceed 30% of the production budget.
- The target capital structure is normally 70–80% debt or contracted/non-dilutive finance and no more than 30% equity, subject to the evidence and economics of the project.
- Gap finance must be secured against conservatively assessed unsold territories, must not be used in isolation and must not exceed 20% of the production budget.
- A completion bond from an acceptable guarantor is mandatory for every financed production.
- The production SPV must be ring-fenced and capable of granting the required security, assignments and control rights.
- No receivable, MG, incentive, producer contribution, deferral or other source may be double-counted.
- Finance is committed only when the relevant binding documents, approvals, funding mechanics and conditions have been verified. An indication, LOI, term sheet, application or verbal assurance is not closed finance.

## 5. Evidence classification
Use only these evidence labels:

| Evidence status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified and executed | Complete, current and executed evidence reviewed; effectiveness confirmed |
| Documented but conditional | Written evidence exists but material conditions remain |
| Draft / negotiation | Non-final document that does not bind all required parties |
| Indicative | Quote, LOI, term sheet, estimate, application or other non-binding signal |
| Represented | Stated by a project party but not independently evidenced |
| Assumed | Analytical assumption only |
| Not received / unable to verify | Evidence is absent or insufficient |

Never infer a favourable fact from silence or missing documentation.

## 6. Closing-status taxonomy
Use these statuses in the Master Closing Matrix:

**Not Received → Under Review → Deficient → Draft → Awaiting Signature → Executed → Satisfied / Waived / Not Applicable**

Executed and satisfied are not synonymous. A document can be executed while a condition, consent, payment, filing, delivery or dependency remains outstanding. A waiver is effective only when the authorised party, scope, conditions and expiry are evidenced.

## 7. Formal greenlight decisions
Every full assessment must make exactly one formal decision:

### GREENLIGHT READY
The closing package is substantially complete; all mandatory gates are satisfied; funding, insurance and bond reliance are evidenced; and no Critical or unresolved High item prevents closing, first draw, production or delivery.

### CONDITIONAL GREENLIGHT
The project may proceed only subject to a finite, specific and manageable set of conditions. Every condition must have an owner, due date, dependency and evidence required for satisfaction. A condition that prevents lawful production, available funding, mandatory insurance or bonding cannot be treated as merely administrative.

### HOLD – MATERIAL CLOSING BLOCKERS
One or more material matters presently prevent funding, bonding, insurance, lawful production, first draw or reliable delivery, but the project may become closable through identified remediation.

### DEVELOPMENT / NOT YET CLOSABLE
The project has fundamental packaging, rights, budget, financing, market, contractual or structural work outstanding and is not yet a closing-stage transaction.

## 8. Score-to-decision mapping

| Readiness score | Default decision candidate | Mandatory qualification |
|---:|---|---|
| 90–100 | GREENLIGHT READY | All mandatory gates satisfied; no Critical or unresolved High blocker |
| 80–89 | CONDITIONAL GREENLIGHT | Remaining CPs are finite, evidenced, owned and achievable before deadline |
| 55–79 | HOLD – MATERIAL CLOSING BLOCKERS | Remediation plan required |
| 0–54 | DEVELOPMENT / NOT YET CLOSABLE | Fundamental work remains |

The score is subordinate to gating issues. A single Critical issue can require HOLD or DEVELOPMENT regardless of score. An analyst must never raise the formal decision merely to match a numerical score.

## 9. Mandatory gates
Assess and record at least:

1. corporate authority, SPV, ownership, KYC and beneficial ownership;
2. chain of title, IP, writers, music and required releases;
3. creative attachments and legally reliable availability;
4. schedule, budget, cashflow, contingency and credible cost to complete;
5. debt facilities, collateral, security, assignments, intercreditor terms and CPs;
6. equity subscriptions, funding evidence, investor rights and waterfall;
7. incentive, grant, co-production and public-funding eligibility and timing;
8. sales agency, distribution, MGs, pre-sales and contracted revenue;
9. insurance binding status, exclusions, endorsements and lender interests;
10. mandatory completion bond, exclusions, overcost mechanics and lender protections;
11. cast, crew, guild, union and employment obligations;
12. legal opinions, consents, waivers and closing certificates;
13. banking, escrow, CAMA, payment directions and funds flow;
14. post, technical delivery, QC and acceptance requirements;
15. litigation, claims, sanctions, regulatory and data/cyber compliance.

## 10. Master institutional closing checklist
Every project must be reconciled against the 243-item Greenlight and Closing Control workbook maintained in `00 Project Governance & Closing Control`. The workbook is the live project control record; these knowledge files provide the assessment methodology.

For each item record:

| Required field | Minimum content |
|---|---|
| Workstream / document / condition | Exact requirement |
| Required or N/A | N/A must include rationale and approval |
| Evidence status | Use the controlled evidence labels |
| Closing status | Use the controlled closing-status taxonomy |
| Responsible owner | Named person and organisation |
| Counterparty | Relevant contracting or approving party |
| Version and date | Current reviewed document |
| Signature / effectiveness | Parties, authority and operative status |
| Dependency | Preceding or linked requirement |
| Due date / long-stop | Exact deadline |
| Risk | Critical / High / Medium / Low |
| CP classification | Debt, equity, insurer, bond or other requirement |
| Evidence to close | Objective proof required for satisfaction |
| Reviewer / approval | Person authorised to mark satisfied or waived |
| Comments and change | Current issue and movement since prior review |

## 12. Operational RAG versus formal decision
Green / Amber / Red may be used for weekly production and lender monitoring. It is an operational signal only and does not replace the formal greenlight decision. Both must be shown where monitoring has begun.

## 13. Weekly audit output
Each weekly review must show:

- current formal decision and readiness score;
- movement since the preceding review;
- evidence received, executed and newly satisfied;
- missing, deficient, unsigned, expired or expiring items;
- Critical and High blockers;
- debt/collateral, equity, insurance and bond status;
- budget, cash, contingency, schedule and cost-to-complete position;
- owner, due date and evidence required for every action;
- decisions required and next closing milestone.

Recipients must come from the approved Stakeholder Register, not automatically from everyone with folder access.

## 14. Time-sensitive verification
Verify at the assessment date:

- law, regulation and enforceability;
- tax incentive and treaty requirements;
- interest rates, FX, pricing and advance availability;
- insurer and completion-guarantor terms;
- counterparty solvency, credit and authority;
- market appetite, sales values and talent bankability;
- sanctions, litigation and regulatory status.

State the verification date, source and any limitation. If live verification is unavailable, classify the matter as unable to verify and do not present it as satisfied.

## 15. Prohibited conclusions
Do not state or imply that:

- a file is satisfactory merely because it is present;
- a draft is executed;
- an executed document is effective when conditions remain;
- a quote means insurance is bound;
- a bond application means a completion guarantee is issued;
- an LOI or term sheet means financing is committed;
- a sales estimate is debt collateral;
- headline MG or incentive value equals bankable net collateral;
- an unresolved Critical issue is cured by a strong aggregate score.
