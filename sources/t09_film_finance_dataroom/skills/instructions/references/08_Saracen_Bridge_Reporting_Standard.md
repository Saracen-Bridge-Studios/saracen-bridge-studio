# Saracen Bridge Analytics — Reporting Standard

**Version:** 1.1  
**Effective date:** 28 August 2026  
**Applies to:** Saracen Bridge Film Finance Analyst reports, dashboards, decision papers and weekly closing communications

## 1. Purpose

Every output must enable a finance, legal, insurance, bond, production or investment decision. Lead with the conclusion and the evidence that supports it. Do not bury closing blockers in narrative.

## 2. Required identity and presentation

- Brand: **Saracen Bridge Analytics**.
- Language: UK English.
- Tone: institutional, commercially direct, precise and concise.
- Word documents: Calibri; title and principal headings 14 pt bold; section headings 11 pt bold; body 11 pt.
- Mark the report **Private & Confidential** and identify the project, SPV, reporting date, review period, preparer and authorised circulation.
- Never mention ChatGPT, AI generation or internal prompting in a client-facing report.
- Use tables where they improve auditability. Keep tables within page margins and repeat header rows across pages.

## 3. Decision first

Page one must show:

1. Formal greenlight decision.
2. Overall readiness score and five dashboard metrics.
3. Change since the previous audit.
4. Critical blockers remaining.
5. Funding gap.
6. Decisions required now.

Use only the four formal decisions defined in the Governance, Greenlight and Evidence Standard. A RAG indicator is operational information and must not replace the formal decision.

## 4. Evidence discipline

Every material conclusion must identify its evidence status and the live document path, filename and version/date where available. Distinguish:

- verified executed fact;
- documented but conditional matter;
- draft or negotiation;
- indication or estimate;
- project-party representation;
- analyst assumption;
- not received or unable to verify.

Do not state that a document is missing unless the reviewed search scope is recorded. Use **Unable to verify within the authorised review scope** where appropriate.

## 5. Blocker presentation

Every Critical or High item must show:

| Field | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Check ID | Stable Master Closing Matrix identifier |
| Workstream | Relevant data-room workstream |
| Finding | Precise defect or unmet condition |
| Consequence | Effect on funding, legal close, insurance, bond, production or delivery |
| Owner | Named responsible person or organisation |
| Due date | Specific date or stated as unassigned |
| Dependency | Preceding requirement or counterparty action |
| Cure evidence | Exact evidence required to close the item |
| Days outstanding | Age from first recorded date |
| Escalation | Required decision or professional confirmation |

Do not use vague remedies such as “finalise documents” or “resolve financing”.

## 6. Finance reporting

Show approved budget, financing required, face-value sources, bankable funding, underwritten haircuts, fees/reserves, funding gap and timing. Identify double counting, circular sources and sources not yet binding. Present all talent figures in USD; state the exchange-rate basis where the project base currency differs.

## 7. Weekly comparison

State:

- documents received or changed;
- items improved, deteriorated, satisfied, waived or newly applicable;
- score and decision movement;
- new and aged blockers;
- agreements unsigned, expiring or approaching long-stop dates;
- next seven-day actions;
- decisions required from recipients.

## 8. Limitations and reliance

Include a concise statement that the report is a management and closing-control recommendation and is not legal, tax, accounting, insurance, completion-bond or investment advice or funding authority. Identify inaccessible folders, unreviewed documents, stale information and matters requiring specialist verification.

## 9. Distribution

Weekly recipients must come only from Active Stakeholder Register entries with Weekly Report = Yes. Respect confidentiality tiers and To/CC designation. Restricted personal, medical, KYC, banking, claims, investigation or privileged detail must not appear in the general report.
