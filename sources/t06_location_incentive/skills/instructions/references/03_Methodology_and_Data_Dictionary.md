# Production Location and Incentive Methodology

## Controlled purpose

Compare single-jurisdiction and split-workstream structures using net present economic value, eligibility, certainty, cash flow, production capability and delivery risk. A headline incentive percentage is never the recommendation by itself.

## Workstream taxonomy

- Development and pre-production.
- Principal photography, second unit and additional photography.
- Stages, backlots, tanks, volumes, workshops, equipment and facilities.
- Picture post, sound post, music, ADR, mixing, mastering, localisation, QC and delivery.
- VFX, CGI, animation, motion capture, simulation, compositing and rendering.
- Physical SFX, stunts and specialist action work.
- Virtual production, LED volume, real-time engines and virtual art departments.
- AI, software, R&D, workforce training and innovation activity where expressly eligible.

Do not treat post, VFX, physical SFX, virtual production or AI/R&D as interchangeable workstreams.

## Incentive taxonomy

- Refundable, non-refundable and transferable tax credits or deductions.
- Cash rebates and expenditure refunds.
- Screen funds, selective grants, soft loans and co-production funds.
- Payroll, employment, training and workforce-development support.
- Regional, municipal, rural and economic-development uplifts.
- VAT, sales-tax, accommodation-tax, customs and import reliefs.
- Infrastructure, sustainability, inward-investment and innovation programmes.

## Financial definitions

- **Headline rate:** Advertised percentage before exclusions, caps, timing, monetisation and incremental costs.
- **Qualifying expenditure:** The part of a budget line that satisfies territorial, category, entity, worker/vendor and programme rules.
- **Gross calculated incentive:** Qualifying expenditure multiplied by the applicable tiered rate after caps.
- **Bankable amount:** Amount a lender is prepared to advance, not the incentive face value.
- **Net realisable benefit:** Gross incentive less financing, transfer, application, audit/legal, entity/compliance and incremental location costs.
- **Present-value benefit:** Net realisable benefit discounted for expected receipt timing.
- **Funding surplus/(gap):** Permanent financing sources less permanent budget uses. Repayable VAT/IVA working capital is excluded.

## Calculation rules

1. Qualifying expenditure = budget line × local-spend percentage × eligibility percentage, subject to category and programme limits.
2. Gross incentive = qualifying expenditure × applicable tiered rate, subject to project and programme caps.
3. Bankable amount = gross incentive × lender advance or realisation percentage.
4. Finance cost = advanced amount × annual rate × months outstanding ÷ 12, plus lender fees.
5. Other costs = transfer haircut + application + audit/legal + local entity/compliance + incremental location costs.
6. Net realisable benefit = gross incentive − finance cost − other costs.
7. Present-value benefit = net realisable benefit ÷ (1 + discount rate)^(months to receipt ÷ 12).
8. Effective benefit rate = present-value benefit ÷ total production budget.
9. Funding surplus/(gap) = permanent sources − permanent uses.

Show the reporting currency, budget version, FX source/date and every material assumption. Reconcile all scenarios to one baseline.

## Eligibility test sequence

For each programme test:

1. Legal and funding status.
2. Eligible project format and ownership/service route.
3. Applicant, local company and taxpayer requirements.
4. Cultural/economic test and certification.
5. Minimum production cost, minimum local spend and territoriality.
6. Pre-approval, application and start-of-spend timing.
7. Resident/non-resident payroll, registered vendor and withholding rules.
8. ATL, cast, related-party, overhead and category caps/exclusions.
9. Per-project, per-episode and annual programme caps.
10. Stacking, double-claim, subsidy-intensity and state-aid restrictions.
11. Audit, filing, certificate and payment process.
12. Refundability, transferability, assignability, tax capacity and lender acceptance.

Use zero rather than a guessed eligible percentage when evidence is insufficient.

## Scenario requirements

- Current-plan or no-incentive baseline.
- Base case.
- Conservative case.
- Downside case when a material variable is uncertain.
- Cash-flowed and non-cash-flowed views where timing matters.
- Split-workstream option where different activities may have different optimum locations.
- FX and timing sensitivity where spend and receipts use different currencies.

## Default location scoring

Scores use a 1–5 scale: 1 materially unsuitable/unverified; 2 weak; 3 viable with caveats; 4 strong; 5 exceptional and well evidenced.

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Net present economic value | 25% |
| Incentive certainty | 15% |
| Cash-flow timing | 10% |
| Crew depth and cost | 10% |
| Facilities and physical production | 8% |
| Post/VFX/animation capability | 8% |
| Creative and location fit | 7% |
| Schedule, weather and daylight | 5% |
| Travel and logistics | 4% |
| Legal, political and currency risk | 4% |
| Data, IP and AI controls | 2% |
| Sustainability | 2% |

Weights may change only for a disclosed project reason and must total 100%.

Default RAG: Red below 2.50; Amber 2.50–3.49; Green 3.50 or above. A critical unresolved eligibility, route, funding, timing, tax-capacity, source or completion dependency overrides a numerical Green.

## Decision statuses

- **INDICATIVE:** Directional view based on incomplete data.
- **PRELIMINARY:** Calculated comparison with material inputs or confirmations outstanding.
- **CONDITIONALLY PREFERRED:** A route leads subject to named decision gates.
- **VALIDATED:** Inputs reconcile and material programme, route, cost, timing and financing matters have suitable written support.

## Minimum output

Lead with the recommended structure, fallback, status and principal commercial reason. Include the top three to five routes; eligible spend; gross and bankable incentive; costs; net and present-value benefit; effective rate; funding gap/surplus; score, RAG and confidence; budget/finance reconciliation; eligibility and timetable; production/post/VFX factors; sensitivities; assumptions and gaps; action plan; adviser checklist; and dated direct sources.

## Source tiers

1. Legislation, regulation, tax authority, government budget and official programme rules.
2. Official film commission, screen fund, application form, official FAQ or payment data.
3. Dated written authority or qualified local-adviser confirmation.
4. Reputable industry/specialist summary for discovery and cross-checking only.
5. Marketing page, social post or search snippet; never sole support.

## Maintenance controls

- Monthly change scan.
- Quarterly full verification of active priority jurisdictions.
- Immediate event review after budgets, elections, announcements, suspensions, guideline changes or material adviser updates.
- No silent overwrites: retain previous value, change date, source and verifier.
- High confidence requires current authoritative evidence and clear operational guidance.
