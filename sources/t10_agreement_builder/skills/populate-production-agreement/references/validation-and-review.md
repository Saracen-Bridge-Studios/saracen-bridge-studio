# Validation and review

## Automated blocking checks

The wrapper verifies bundled asset hashes, the master fixed-package fingerprint, exact field IDs, unchanged input clause/context/type metadata, allowed status values, required sources, non-empty active values, plain numeric types, finite values, rate ranges, whole-number tier references, single-line text and an explicit reason for Not applicable. It refuses to overwrite the master, blank workbook, input file or an existing output version.

It checks Producer/Talent plus Financier pools total 100% and, where all four explicit participant percentages are present, their sum is 100%. It checks all nine funding line amounts against the funding total when every amount is entered or expressly Not applicable; permits two cents of aggregate rounding difference. It checks the signed funding-minus-budget difference and the agreement's two budget entries where present. Proposed values enter these calculations only when included in the output. A draft containing proposed terms requires DRAFT in its version field.

The population engine modifies text inside named controls only and compares the entire DOCX package after normalising the control text. A change to styles, clauses, content controls, tables, numbering, headers or other package content fails that comparison.

## Limits and manual checks

Warnings are not a pass for signing. Incomplete fields keep their placeholders. A source string is not proof of execution. Automated sums cannot detect an incentive hidden inside a partner package, a bridge counted twice, an unapproved budget case, disputed rights ownership, tax eligibility or an ambiguous fee base. A partly specified profit pool or waterfall cannot pass a substantive completeness review.

Review the actual source documents, execution status, conditions, issuer/provider legal identity, authority, production SPV, rights transfers, talent attachments, approved budget/schedule, timing of each cash receipt, restricted repayment sources, gross/net incentive and MG proceeds, financing costs, FX and one-cent rounding differences. Review fee bases, caps, deferrals, participant shares, waterfall tiers, credit and control rights, tax, collection account and bond/insurance evidence.

Keep money raised at a parent company separate from a project SPV commitment. Keep preliminary tax models separate from project-specific advice. Preserve conflicting source cases in the report until the user selects a working case. Do not rewrite the governing-law provisions as part of filling fields.

Render and inspect all pages before delivering. The template's formatting and fixed wording remain static, but changing field lengths can change pagination. Record the visual review separately: the script never claims it has performed it. For legal review use current primary sources and distinguish commercial recommendations from legal conclusions; do not certify the master as legally compliant based on technical validation.

## Completion report contents

Record project/draft/master version, source register, populated and status counts, structural check outcome, arithmetic checks, unresolved terms with Field IDs and suggested owners, conflicting evidence, proposed assumptions and next decisions. Identify separate legal/tax/execution checks without describing a discussion draft as ready for signature.
